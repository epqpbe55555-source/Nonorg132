Aluminum (Al) — Inorganic Pharmaceutical Chemistry · 3rd Stage

Overview

The key facts and rules — not the full lecture. Everything below is worth remembering on its own.

Element basics

  • Atomic number 13, symbol Al, classified as a post-transition metal.
  • Soft, durable, lightweight — used in construction, aircraft, packaging.

Biological role & distribution

  • Body contains ~35 mg of Al³⁺: ~50% lungs, ~50% skeleton.
  • No known biological role — the body actively excludes it.
  • Minimal gut absorption; kidneys excrete most of it quickly.
  • Bone acts as a slow-release sink; the blood–brain barrier normally blocks brain entry.
  • Competes with Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺, Fe²⁺/³⁺ (similar ionic radius/charge).
  • At pH 7.4, forms barely soluble Al(OH)₃.

Toxicity & accumulation

  • Accumulates with IV use, dialysis, impaired kidney function.
  • Dialysis dementia (Alfrey, 1972): encephalopathy after ≥15 months hemodialysis.
  • Symptoms: speech disorders, bone mineralization problems, dementia signs.
  • Prevented today with Al³⁺-free dialysate / reverse osmosis.

Pharmaceutical uses (4 categories)

  • Adjuvant — Al³⁺ salts, FDA-approved, slow-release antigen depot (anthrax, diphtheria, Hib, hepatitis A/B, Lyme, pertussis, tetanus).
  • Antacid — Al(OH)₃ is amphoteric, neutralizes HCl, no CO₂ side effect.
  • Phosphate binder — Al(OH)₃ / aluminosilicates bind phosphate as AlPO₄, excreted in feces.
  • Antiperspirant — AlCl₃ / aluminum hexahydrate plug the sweat duct.

Key reactions

Al(OH)₃ + 3HCl → AlCl₃ + 3H₂O
NaHCO₃ + HCl → NaCl + H₂O + CO₂
  • Al(OH)₃ route avoids CO₂ (no bloating); NaHCO₃ route can cause alkaline urine in large doses.
  • Constipation from Al(OH)₃ is often offset with Mg²⁺ antacids + simethicone.

Alginate raft (GERD relief)

  • Alginates: natural polysaccharides from brown seaweed.
  • Raft = alginic acid + NaHCO₃ + magnesium trisilicate + Al(OH)₃.
  • Trapped CO₂ makes the gel float like a raft, physically blocking reflux.

Al(OH)₃ is amphoteric. This route produces no gas, so it avoids bloating — but Al(OH)₃ is known to cause constipation, often managed with Mg²⁺ antacids + simethicone.

Alginate raft formulation — mechanism

Three steps that turn a swallowed liquid into a floating protective raft.

Alginate salts and alginic acid contact gastric HCl and precipitate into a low-density, viscous gel matrix inside the acidic stomach.

Al(OH)₃ as a phosphate binder — pathway

Follow Al(OH)₃ from the stomach to excretion.

In the acidic stomach (pH ~1), aluminum hydroxide Al(OH)₃ dissociates into free Al³⁺ ions.
